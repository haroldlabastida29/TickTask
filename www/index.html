The Problem We Solve

Students struggle to keep track of academic tasks and deadlines because busy schedules cause information to scatter across different sources such as group chats, class portals, notebooks, and paper notes. Forgetting a critical task or deadline directly impacts academic responsibilities and daily schedules.

Target Users

  • Working and Busy Students: Individuals balancing jobs or heavy extracurricular workloads who need a quick way to track tasks on tight schedules.
  • Students Prone to Forgetting: Learners who lose track of tasks due to stress, multitasking, or juggling multiple subjects simultaneously.
  • College Students: Students looking for a straightforward, reliable system dedicated to managing academic milestones.

Core System Features & Architecture

🔐

Centralized Authentication

Secure accounts where each user's tasks are stored separately in the database.

⚡

Hybrid Data Structures

Dynamic arrays for sequential lists combined with Hash Maps using task map at task id for instant constant time lookups.

🔍

Linear Search & Filters

Quickly search keywords or filter records instantly by status, category, or priority level.

📊

Dual-Criteria Sorting

Automatically orders tasks by deadline timestamps and priority weights where High is valued at 3, Medium is valued at 2, and Low is valued at 1.
